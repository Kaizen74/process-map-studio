The visual language

The manual is quiet grey; what happens is ink; colour is spent only on what needs a decision.

One channel, one meaning
ColourKind of impact of a gap. Nothing else. Line styleWhere work happens: solid in the system of record, dashed for a deviation the system records, dotted off-system. ShapeKind of thing: box = step, hexagon = exception ID, triangle = missing record, page = unofficial tool. WidthShare of cases. LengthMinutes, on time views only. AreaHours lost a month. Filled or hollowMeasured or evidenced (filled) against believed or absent (hollow).
Impact: always a glyph and a word with the colour
Control exposureA control or record weakened. Never grouped or hidden. Time impactMaterial delay for the customer. Capacity drainEffort spent with little consequence downstream. WorkaroundAn unofficial tool or route. Not a fault: may be one to keep. Designed workOnly the designed minutes; never a finding.
